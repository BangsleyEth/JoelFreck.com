Your instructor

I’m Joel Freck.

I’m a Halifax house DJ, producer, and electronic music teacher. I won Beatport and Anjunabeats’ Beats In School competition and a Wavo remix contest for Laidback Luke through Mixmash Records. I went on to share the stage with Luke in San Diego and Las Vegas. My collaboration “Dawn” was released on Armada’s A State of Trance label and featured in Armin van Buuren’s 2020 Year Mix.

I also held a three-year Pacifico residency in Halifax with PT93 and have played support for James Kennedy and Dave Summer. I bring what I’ve learned from writing records and playing rooms into every session.

Lesson FAQ.

Can I start with just one lesson?

Yes. You can book a single 60-minute session at any experience level. If you’re exploring electronic music schools in Halifax, a private lesson is a way to try DJing or music production and see what you’d like to learn next.

Can I learn to DJ without owning a controller?

Yes. For in-person DJ lessons, we can use my laptop and USB DJ controller. Tell me what kind of setup you’d like to perform on and we’ll focus on the skills you’ll use with it. For online lessons, tell me what gear you already have so we can plan around it.

Where are DJ and production lessons held?

I teach in person in Halifax and Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, and online. If you’re booking in person, include your neighbourhood so we can work out where to meet. If you’re farther out, I’ll confirm any travel cost before we book.

What should I bring to a Logic Pro lesson?

A Mac with Logic Pro lets you follow along. If you have an unfinished track, bring your Logic Pro project or a rough export and we can work through the arrangement and mix together.
