04Social content · Creative direction

Pacifico

20 → 300–400

Regular attendance · Within the first month

Making the promotion match the experience.

Joel and PT93 turned quiet nights at a jazz-led venue into regular house-music crowds. They listened to guests, adjusted the music and offered a free first hour. Joel filmed and edited promotional videos, created posters in Canva and Photoshop, and used Instagram stories and reels to show the room’s energy.

It brought college students and an older crowd into the same room. They went on to hold the Friday and Saturday residency for three years.
